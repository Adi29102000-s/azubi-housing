🏢 Anmeldung Survival Guide

Practical tips to help you find an Anmeldung appointment faster. Availability and booking rules vary by city.

🔥 Check Early for Cancelled Appointments
Appointment systems vary by city. Check the booking page early in the morning and throughout the day, as cancelled appointments may become available. Some offices also release new appointments at specific times.
🌐 Check Multiple Office Locations
If your city has several Bürgeramt locations, check each one. Availability can differ between offices, and another location may have an earlier appointment.
📋 Documents You May Need:
1️⃣ Your valid passport or accepted identity document
2️⃣ Your rental contract (Mietvertrag), if requested
3️⃣ Wohnungsgeberbestätigung: the official confirmation from your landlord or housing provider
💡 Important:
Anmeldung procedures, appointment systems, required documents and appointment-release times can differ between German cities. Always check the official website of your local Bürgeramt before your visit.
