🎓 German Exam Survival Guide

Essential exam rules for international students.

1st Attempt: Main Exam
Your regular exam attempt. If you do not pass, the applicable examination regulations determine whether and when you can take a retake.
2nd Attempt: Retake ⚠️
A retake may be available after a failed attempt. The timing and number of attempts depend on your university, program and examination regulations.
Final Attempt 🚨
Some programs have a final permitted attempt. Failing it can have serious consequences, including loss of the right to continue in that examination subject. Exact rules are university- and program-specific.
💡 Important Safety Nets to Know:

1️⃣ Prüfungsabmeldung: If your examination regulations allow it, withdraw before the applicable deadline so the attempt is not counted.

2️⃣ Krankmeldung: If you are ill, follow your university's procedure immediately. Requirements for medical certificates vary by university.

3️⃣ Härtefallantrag: Some universities/programs provide hardship or exceptional-case procedures. Availability and requirements are specific to the applicable examination regulations.

📌 Important:
There is no single nationwide rule that every German university gives exactly three attempts or an automatic fourth attempt. Always check your own Prüfungsordnung and contact your examination office before relying on an exception.
📲 Student Counselling Services

For exam-specific deadlines and attempt rules, your university's official Prüfungsordnung and examination office are the authoritative sources.
