How the download works
This website cannot directly download Google's offline-map files. Use the buttons above to open your destination in Google Maps. Then use Google's Offline Maps feature to save the area on your phone.
1
Search your destination.
Enter any German city, town or village in the search box.
2
Tap Preview Sector.
Your selected destination will open directly in Google Maps.
3
Make sure you are online.
Use Wi-Fi or mobile data before travelling.
4
Open Google Maps.
Open your profile/menu and select Offline Maps.
5
Select your own map.
Choose the area you need and make sure it includes your destination, accommodation, station, airport or university.
6
Download the area.
Download the offline area while you still have an internet connection.
7
Test your offline map.
Turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data and check that the important area is available offline.
