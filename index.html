i Finish the Download in Google Maps
Do this before you fly. Download your map while you still have internet (home Wi-Fi or Indian data roaming). Once you land in Germany with no signal, you can still navigate freely.

Open the Google Maps app on your phone while you still have a data connection. If you don't have it installed, install it from the Play Store or App Store first.

Make sure Wi-Fi is on, or use your Indian SIM's data roaming — the map file is large and needs internet to download once.

If you tapped Prepare Offline Map above, Google Maps already opens at the right spot — skip to step 3.

Otherwise, search for your city in the Maps search bar and tap the result that shows , Germany to drop a pin on it.

Tap your profile picture (top-right) to open the menu, then select Offline maps.

On older Android versions, tap the three-line hamburger menu on the left instead.

Tap Select your own map. A blue rectangle appears over the map — drag and resize it so it fully covers your city boundary, your airport, and your train station.

Tap Download. Wait for the file to save to your phone storage. You'll see it listed under "Offline maps" when it's done.

Repeat this for every city on your trip (airport city + your university town). Saved maps stay usable for 30 days, then update automatically when you're online again.
