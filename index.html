Ausbildung · Germany · Housing

Where Azubis
actually get a room

Student dorms turn trainees away. These are the systems built for you instead — with the application page for each one.

Check this before you apply anywhere

  • Most Azubi dorms accept ages 16–26 only
  • You need a signed Ausbildungsvertrag, or one starting soon
  • Your training company must be in that city
  • University students are not eligible — apply to Studierendenwerk instead

Start with these three

Work through them in this order. The first two are cheap and hard to get. The third is expensive and always available.

01
Azubiwerk & Jugendwohnheime

Dorms built specifically for trainees. Furnished rooms, shared flats, often with staff on site. Cheapest option by a wide margin. Hamburg has the most developed network; Kolping runs houses nationwide.

Azubiwerk Hamburg ↗ Kolping Azubiwohnen ↗
02
Ask your training company

Werkswohnungen — company housing. Common in nursing, hospitals, airports and heavy industry. Nobody advertises this. Ask HR directly during onboarding, before you start looking anywhere else. It costs you one email.

03
WG-Gesucht

Germany's main shared-flat platform. Always has listings, always competitive. Write in German, answer everything the ad asks for, and never send money before signing.

If the rent doesn't fit your salary

Two government payments exist for exactly this. Both are applications you make yourself — nobody will offer them to you.

Berufsausbildungsbeihilfe (BAB)

Monthly support from the Agentur für Arbeit for trainees living away from home whose parents can't cover costs. Apply through the employment agency.

Find the application ↗

Wohngeld

General rent subsidy from your local Bürgeramt. This is the route if you're not eligible for BAB — for example on a second Ausbildung.

Find the application ↗

Walk away if you see this

  • Money requested before a viewing or a signed contract
  • Landlord says they're abroad and will post the keys
  • Rent far below everything else in that city
  • No photos of the inside
  • Pressure to decide today because "others are waiting"

Check every link before you rely on it
