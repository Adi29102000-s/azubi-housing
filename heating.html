🔥 Smart Heating Rules

Best practice: Keep a reasonably stable temperature instead of constantly switching the heating completely on and off.
  • 🌡️ Around 18–21°C is usually comfortable for living spaces.
  • 🛏️ Bedrooms can generally be cooler.
  • 🚪 Close doors between heated and cooler rooms.
  • 🪟 Do not permanently leave windows tilted open while heating.
  • 💨 Ventilate quickly with the windows fully open.
  • 🧱 Keep radiators unobstructed by furniture or thick curtains.

💨 Ventilation

What is Stoßlüften?

Stoßlüften means opening the windows fully for a short period to replace humid indoor air with fresh air.

  • Open the window completely.
  • Usually ventilate for several minutes.
  • Close the window afterwards.
  • Avoid leaving the window tilted open for hours during winter.
Tip: Cross-ventilation through windows on opposite sides of an apartment can make ventilation much faster.
The Legal Liability Trap

Heating and ventilation can matter when landlords and tenants discuss moisture or mould damage.

However, responsibility depends on the circumstances, the building, ventilation behaviour, heating behaviour and the actual cause of the moisture problem.

Don't panic: If you notice mould or persistent moisture, document the problem and inform your landlord promptly rather than trying to determine liability yourself.
Post-Shower Protocol

Showers can create a large amount of humidity, especially in small bathrooms.

  • 🚿 Remove excess water from wet surfaces where practical.
  • 💨 Ventilate the bathroom after showering.
  • 🚪 Keep bathroom humidity from spreading through the apartment.
  • 🪟 Use short, effective ventilation rather than leaving windows tilted for long periods.
Simple routine: Shower → ventilate → close the window → allow the room to dry.

❄️ Winter Heating Checklist

  • ✔️ Check that radiators are not blocked.
  • ✔️ Keep rooms at a reasonable temperature.
  • ✔️ Ventilate briefly and effectively.
  • ✔️ Watch for condensation around windows.
  • ✔️ Keep an eye on unusual dampness or mould.
  • ✔️ Report building-related moisture problems early.

⚠️ Important
