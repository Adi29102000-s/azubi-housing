The Unwritten Laws
💨 What is Stoßlüften?
You cannot leave windows tilted open (*Kippen*) all day. This wastes energy and cools the walls down. Instead, practice Stoßlüften (Shock Ventilation): open your windows completely wide for 5 to 10 minutes, 2-3 times a day. This exchanges the humid air instantly without losing structural wall heat.
⚖️ The Legal Liability Trap
Under German tenancy regulations, moisture and mold prevention is a legal obligation for tenants. If mold develops because you kept windows permanently shut, the landlord will hold you liable for the commercial restoration bill and forfeit your security deposit (*Kaution*).
🚿 Post-Shower Protocol
Every single time you take a hot shower or boil water while cooking, you must wipe moisture off the bathroom/kitchen window frames and open the window wide immediately. Leaving standing humidity inside a closed flat will guarantee mold formation within 14 days.
