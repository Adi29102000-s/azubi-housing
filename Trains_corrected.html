Germany Transit Essentials 🚆

The essential apps to help international students plan trains, buses, trams and walking routes across Germany.

🚆
1. DB Navigator
Plan journeys on Deutsche Bahn services, including ICE, IC, RE and RB trains, and check live connections and disruptions.
BEST FOR TRAINS
 iPhone / iPad 🤖 Android
💡 If you bought your Deutschlandticket through Deutsche Bahn, you can manage it in the DB Navigator app. Other providers may use their own ticket apps.
🚌
2. Öffi — Public Transport
A useful Android public-transport app for checking local buses, trams, U-Bahn and other public-transport connections in supported regions.
ANDROID PUBLIC TRANSPORT
 iOS Alternative: Citymapper 🤖 Öffi for Android
💡 Öffi is an Android app. The iPhone button above provides a separate public-transport alternative rather than an iOS version of Öffi.
📍
3. Google Maps
Useful for door-to-door directions, walking routes, places, local transport connections and navigating unfamiliar towns.
BEST FOR DOOR-TO-DOOR NAVIGATION
 iPhone / iPad 🤖 Android

Quick tips for students 💡

🚄
Long-distance trains

Use DB Navigator to check ICE and IC connections and live travel updates.

🚌
Local transport

Check the transport app that supports your city or region for local buses and trams.

📍
Finding an address

Google Maps is useful when you need walking directions from a station to your destination.
