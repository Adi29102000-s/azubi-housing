Germany SIM Card Guide

Get mobile data without falling into a 2-year trap

1. The 2-Year Contract Trap

Walking into major outlets like O2, Vodafone, or Telekom usually locks you into a mandatory 24-month contract. Breaking it early if you relocate out of Germany is incredibly tedious, and the automated billing cycle will continue draining your bank account.

2. The Safe Strategy: Prepaid Flexibility

To avoid massive cancellation fees, only opt for contract-free monthly rolling packages which you can dynamically cancel or pause every 30 days.

3. Best Initial Setup Options

  • ALDI Talk / Lidl Connect: Cheap physical prepaid cards available immediately at supermarket billing counters. No hidden dynamic commitments.
  • Fraenk: A premium, zero-commitment digital app eSIM that runs cleanly on the high-speed Telekom infrastructure. Managed entirely within their application.

4. Early Cancellation Template

If you signed a long-term plan and are permanently moving away, you can use your Abmeldung (City Deregistration) to invoke an extraordinary cancellation under Section 46 of the TKG. Use this template:

Kündigungsschreiben (German) 
Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,

hiermit kündige ich meinen Mobilfunkvertrag außerordentlich zum nächstmöglichen Zeitpunkt nach § 46 TKG wegen meiner dauerhaften Ausreise aus Deutschland.

Kundennummer: [Account Number]
Rufnummer: [Mobile Number]

Meine offizielle Abmeldebestätigung füge ich als Nachweis bei. Bitte bestätigen Sie mir das Vertragsenddatum.

Mit freundlichen Grüßen,
[Your Name]
