🧮 International grade conversion

The modified Bavarian Formula is used by institutions such as TUM to convert grades earned outside Germany. The conversion uses the maximum grade, the lowest passing grade, and the grade achieved in the original grading system. The result is an estimate; official conversions depend on the applicable university and program regulations.
